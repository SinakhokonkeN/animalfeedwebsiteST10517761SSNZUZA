Amashazi Animal Feed Suppliers supplies feed for poultry, cattle, goats, sheep and pigs, along with selected animal vitamins and supplements.

Based in the Bulwer area of KwaZulu-Natal, the business serves small-scale farmers, poultry farmers, commercial farmers and livestock owners.

Whether you are planning a regular feed purchase or asking about a new category, start with the animals you keep. Prepare a list, include your quantities and confirm the right product details with the supplier.
