Privacy & your information

Your details.
Handled with care.

Understand what this website stores and what happens when you prepare a request.

Your quote list

Feed category IDs and quantities are saved in your browser’s local storage so you can return to them. Clear the list on the quote page or clear this site’s browser data to remove them. Contact details and message contents are not stored in browser storage.

Forms and request delivery

Preparing a message validates your details and creates a summary on your device. It does not send email. If request recording is available, the separate “Record request” button sends the form to the website server. A successful receipt means the server has recorded the request; it does not confirm stock, pricing or email delivery.

When a business email address has been configured, “Open email draft” opens your email app with the message. You must review and send it yourself. A failed or unavailable recording service leaves the summary available to download or copy.

Information supplied

Forms request your name, email, optional phone number and the details needed to respond. Include only information relevant to your enquiry. Do not include payment information or sensitive personal information. Recorded requests should be accessed only by the business operator and retained only as long as needed to deal with the enquiry.

Google Maps

The map is off by default. Choosing “Load Google map” connects to Google and may share information such as your IP address and browser details with Google. You can unload the map at any time. Google’s privacy policy applies to its services.

Other preferences

A motion preference may be saved in this browser. There are no advertising or analytics scripts in this build. The website does not ask for your device location.

Questions or removal requests

Use the contact form to prepare a question or a request concerning your information. Direct business contact details are being confirmed.
